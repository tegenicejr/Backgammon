How to Play Backgammon

🎯

Objective

Move all 15 checkers into your home board and bear them off the board before your opponent.

🔄

Movement

White moves counter-clockwise toward point 1. Black moves clockwise toward point 24. Doubles give 4 moves!

⚔️

Hitting & The Bar

Landing on a single enemy checker (a blot) sends it to the central Bar. Checkers on the bar must re-enter first!

🏁

Bearing Off

Once all 15 checkers are in your inner home quadrant, you can roll the dice to remove them to the right-side tray.
