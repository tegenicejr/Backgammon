How to Play

1. Goal

Move all 15 checkers into your Home Board and bear them off before your opponent does.

2. Movement & Blots

White moves counter-clockwise (24 -> 1). An isolated single checker is a "blot". Landing on an opponent's blot hits it to the Bar.

3. Doubles

Rolling matching numbers gives you 4 moves of that value instead of 2!
