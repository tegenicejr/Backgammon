How to Play

1. Objective & Path

White moves counter-clockwise in a U-shape towards Point 1. Bring all 15 checkers into your Home Board and bear them off before your opponent does.

2. Hit & The Bar

Landing on an isolated single checker (blot) hits it! It is sent to the central Bar and must re-enter from the furthest quadrant.

3. Making Points (Block)

Stacking 2 or more of your checkers secures that point. Opponents cannot land there and must leap over it.

4. Doubles (4 Moves!)

Rolling matching dice gives you 4 moves of that value instead of 2! A huge chance to make a comeback.
